ACCESS GUIDE

From Miaoli Station
to Nara Select

First visit? Remember three things: Yingcai Road rear exit, the left skybridge on the second floor, and the “Locomotive Village” signs.

Google Maps directionsAsk on LINE

Walking route from the station

Follow these five steps after leaving the train. You do not need to enter through the main entrance of Miaoli Railway Museum first.

🚉
1. Look for “Exit”Follow the exit signs inside the station to leave the platform.
↗️
2. Go to the rear exit (Yingcai Road)Do not head toward the front station. Follow signs for the Yingcai Road rear exit.
🌉
3. Take the left skybridge on level 2At the second floor of the rear station, use the skybridge on the left and head toward the spiral tower.
🏘️
4. Walk toward Railway VillageFollow the park signs marked “Locomotive Village”.
⭐
5. Arrive at Nara SelectFind No. 37 Railway Village, Miaoli City—that is Nara Select.
The on-site sign says “Locomotive Village,” while the shop address is “No. 37 Railway Village.” These are two different names.

Driving & parking

The park has no private car park. Use paid parking near the station, then walk to the shop.

Rear-station area

Paid car parks and paid street spaces are available. After parking, walk from the rear-station side.

Front-station area

Paid car parks are also available. After parking, cross the station toward the Yingcai Road rear exit and follow the route above.

※ Spaces, prices and opening status depend on on-site signs. Neither Nara Select nor Miaoli Railway Museum guarantees parking availability.

Easiest landmark
