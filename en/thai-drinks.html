PLAN YOUR VISIT

Stop by for a drink

Nara Select is at No. 37 Railway Village, near Miaoli Railway Museum and within walking distance of the rear exit of Miaoli Station.

🚉 By train

Use the Yingcai Road rear exit, take the left skybridge on the second floor, and head toward the spiral tower.

🏘️ Follow the signs

Follow signs for “Locomotive Village,” then look for No. 37 Railway Village.

🚗 By car

The park has no private car park. Paid parking lots and street spaces are available around both sides of the station.

How do I walk from Miaoli Station?

Do not use the front exit. Take the Yingcai Road rear exit, cross the left skybridge toward the spiral tower, then follow the “Locomotive Village” signs.

Where is the shop?

Nara Select is at No. 37 Railway Village, Miaoli City.

Availability varies daily. If you are making a special trip, please ask us on LINE first.
