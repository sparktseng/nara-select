What customers often mention

Reviews often praise the rich brown-sugar flavour of the traditional shaved ice, especially the tapioca pearls and taro balls. Some customers also recommend the light, savoury oden broth. Reasonable prices, a clean and comfortable setting, and friendly staff are frequently mentioned.
