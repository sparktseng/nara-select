About the shop

Hei Xin Tang is at Railway Village Store No. 6. Its signature brown-sugar starch jelly is made in-house, alongside traditional shaved ice, tofu pudding, grass-jelly desserts and sweet soups. Seasonal items such as oden vary by day.

What to eat here

The signature is brown-sugar starch-jelly mixed shaved ice, pairing aromatic brown sugar with the shop’s house-made starch jelly. Other combinations include rice noodles, grass jelly, red beans and mung beans, plus tofu pudding, grass-jelly milk dessert and sweet soups. Seasonal oden varies by day.

What customers often mention

Reviews often praise the rich brown-sugar flavour of the traditional shaved ice, especially the tapioca pearls and taro balls. Some customers also recommend the light, savoury oden broth. Reasonable prices, a clean and comfortable setting, and friendly staff are frequently mentioned.
