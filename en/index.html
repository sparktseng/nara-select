MIAOLI RAILWAY MUSEUM

First time at Miaoli Railway Museum?

Store No. 5 is in Railway Village inside Miaoli Railway Museum, a relaxed stop for families, railway fans and travellers.

🚂 See the Trains

Explore displayed railway vehicles and activities such as the park train.

🐱 Taiwan Railway Cat Office

Register on site for timed entry. Please check the museum’s daily notice for availability.

🚉 Enter from the Rear Station

The entrance is on the rear-station side of Miaoli Station. Head to Railway Village to find us.

※ Admission, facilities and opening hours may change. Check the latest museum notice before visiting.
