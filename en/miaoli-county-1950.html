The Miaoli District Office building in 1937. It helps us understand the official architecture of the period, but the chain from district office to the first county government site has not yet been fully proven by land records.

Place one: the former district office

After World War II, the Miaoli area belonged to Hsinchu County. Following local efforts and a territorial reorganisation in 1950, Miaoli County was restored on 25 October. An official cultural record states that the county government used the former Miaoli District Office and that its former director, Deng Zhongyan, became the first county magistrate.

This was the administrative workplace. Whether it occupied exactly the same building and parcel as the earlier Japanese-era district office is still being checked against historic land and property records.

Place two: Miaoli Theater

The inauguration ceremony was held at Miaoli Theater on the same day. The theater was the public ceremonial venue, not the county government’s everyday office.

Interviews and an official veterinary clinic directory point to today’s No. 29, Zhigong Road, but the 1950 address, parcel and continuity of the present building still require primary documentation.

What we do not fill in

The official record mentions large celebrations around the county, but the original newspapers for 24–27 October 1950 have not yet been obtained. We therefore do not invent parade routes, programmes, guest lists or speeches. An oral account that the theater was chosen for lack of a large hall also remains unconfirmed.

Confirmed：Date of establishment; former district office as workplace; Deng Zhongyan as first magistrate; theater as inauguration venue.
Still under verification：Exact historical parcels, building history, ceremony details and why the theater was selected.

1950.10.25

Miaoli County Government was formally established on 25 October 1950. Administration began at the former Miaoli District Office, while the inauguration of the first county magistrate, Deng Zhongyan, took place at Miaoli Theater.
