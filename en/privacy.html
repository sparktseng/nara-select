Privacy & website analytics

How nara5.tw uses Google Analytics.

What we collect

After consent, we collect page views, general device and browser information, and clicks on LINE, Google Maps, Instagram, language and internal navigation links.

What we do not collect

Analytics does not send names, phone numbers, LINE accounts, order details, payment data or message contents. Do not place personal data in URLs.

Cookies and choice

Analytics is off by default. It loads only after you select “Allow analytics”; declining keeps it off. Use the button below to choose again.

Third-party service

Analytics data is processed by Google Analytics. See Google’s explanation of how it uses data from partner sites. Google
