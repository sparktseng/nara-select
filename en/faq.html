First time at Nara Select?

The most useful answers before your visit.

Where is Nara Select?

Nara Select is at No. 37 Railway Village, Miaoli City.

How do I walk from Miaoli Station?

Use the Yingcai Road rear exit, take the left skybridge on level 2 toward the spiral tower, then follow the “Locomotive Village” signs.

Is there parking at the park?

The park has no dedicated car park. Paid car parks and metered spaces are available around the front and rear station areas; check conditions on site.

Are Thai drinks and snacks available every day?

Availability depends on daily preparation and the shop. Ask on LINE before making a special trip.

Can Thai goods be reserved?

Products, colours and quantities change. Ask on LINE about a category, but current in-store availability applies.

Are “Locomotive Village” and “Railway Village” the same name?

On-site park signs say “Locomotive Village”; the shop address is No. 37 Railway Village. Use both names when following the route.
